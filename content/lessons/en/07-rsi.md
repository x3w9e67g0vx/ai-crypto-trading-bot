## What RSI shows

**RSI** (Relative Strength Index) compares the strength of recent gains and losses and gives a number from 0 to 100. It is usually calculated over 14 candles.

- **Above 70** — overbought zone: the price rose fast.
- **Below 30** — oversold zone: the price fell fast.
- **Around 50** — balance.

## How to use it

Buying at RSI 85 means entering after a strong spike, when many are ready to take profit. The risk of a pullback is higher than usual.

Shorting at RSI 20 is the same in reverse: the market has already dropped a lot and may bounce.

## The trap

Overbought is not a "sell" signal. In a strong trend RSI can stay above 70 for weeks while the price keeps rising. RSI works better together with the trend: in an uptrend, a dip of RSI to 40–50 is often a better entry than 85.
