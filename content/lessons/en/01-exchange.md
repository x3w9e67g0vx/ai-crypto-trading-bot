## The order book

An exchange is where buyers meet sellers. Buyers post orders like "I'll buy at no more than X", sellers post "I'll sell at no less than Y". All of these orders live in the **order book**.

## Bid and ask

- **Bid** — the best price someone is willing to buy at right now.
- **Ask** — the best price someone is willing to sell at right now.
- **Spread** — the gap between ask and bid.

A market order fills immediately at the best opposite price: a buy at the ask, a sell at the bid. That is why buying and instantly selling loses you the spread.

## Fees

The exchange charges a fee on every trade. In VEYDRANOX it is **0.1%** on opening and the same on closing. A 1,000 USDT trade costs about 2 USDT in fees round trip. Small frequent trades eat your results faster than you think.

Prices in the game are real Binance quotes; the money is virtual.
