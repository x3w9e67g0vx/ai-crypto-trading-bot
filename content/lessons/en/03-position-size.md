## The key question is "how much", not "where"

Even a good forecast can fail. Professionals first decide how much they are willing to lose if they are wrong, and only then pick the size.

## The 1–2% rule

Risk no more than **1–2% of the portfolio** on one trade. Risk is not the position size, it is the amount you lose if the stop is hit.

- Portfolio: 10,000 USDT.
- 1% risk: 100 USDT.
- Stop 5% from entry.
- Position size: 100 / 0.05 = **2,000 USDT**.

## Why it works

Five losing trades in a row at 1% each is about −5%, easy to win back. Five losing trades with the whole portfolio is the end of your season.

## Game limits

In VEYDRANOX you can hold at most 50% of the portfolio in one pair. That is a ceiling, not advice: half your money in one coin is very aggressive.
