## Your main opponent is you

Most trading mistakes are not about indicators but about emotions. The three most common:

- **FOMO** (fear of missing out) — entering after a strong rally because "everyone is making money". Usually that is buying the top.
- **Revenge trading** — right after a loss, opening a bigger trade to win it back. That is how one loss becomes a streak.
- **Overtrading** — trading for the sake of trading. Each trade costs a fee, and the more you make, the more randomness you add.

## What helps

- A plan before entry: why I enter, where the stop is, where the target is.
- A pause after two losing trades in a row.
- A journal: after each closed trade, read the review — what the indicators showed and what the AIs thought.

## Why the game has a limit

VEYDRANOX allows at most 20 trades a day. It is not a restriction but a hint: good trades don't come every five minutes.
