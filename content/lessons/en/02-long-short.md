## Long

A **long** is a bet on the price going up. Buy now, sell later at a higher price. Bought BTC at 100, sold at 110 — you made 10% minus fees.

## Short

A **short** is a bet on the price going down. You effectively borrow a coin, sell it now, then buy it back cheaper and return it. Sold at 100, bought back at 90 — you made 10%.

## Why a short is riskier

- A long's loss is limited: the price can't fall below zero.
- A short's loss is not: the price can go up 2, 3, 10 times.

There is no leverage in the game: both long and short are 1x. Still, a short against a strong rally can lose more than you put in. That is why a stop-loss matters even more for shorts.

## When to use which

Long when you think the price will rise, short when you think it will fall. If you are not sure, the best trade is often no trade.
