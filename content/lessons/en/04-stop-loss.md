## Stop-loss

A **stop-loss (SL)** is a price at which the position closes automatically to limit the loss. You decide it in advance, with a cool head, not while the price is crashing.

## Take-profit

A **take-profit (TP)** is a price at which the position closes in profit. It protects you from greed: "just a bit longer" often ends with the profit gone.

## Where to place them

- For a long: SL below entry, TP above.
- For a short: SL above entry, TP below.
- Put the stop where your idea is proven wrong, not at a random "−5%".

## Risk-reward

With a stop 2% from entry and a take-profit at 4%, the ratio is 1:2. With such a ratio you can be wrong in half of your trades and still be in profit.

In the game SL/TP trigger on the live Binance price. If the exchange is unavailable, the stop fills at the first fresh price after it comes back — which may be worse than your level.
