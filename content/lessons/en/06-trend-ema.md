## The trend is your friend

A **trend** is the direction the price moves most of the time. Trading with the trend is easier than against it: against the trend you are betting the market is about to turn, and timing a reversal is very hard.

## Moving average

A **moving average** is the average price over the last N candles. It smooths out noise and shows the overall direction.

An **EMA** (exponential) gives more weight to recent candles, so it reacts to changes faster.

## Two EMAs

- **EMA 12** — fast, follows the price closely.
- **EMA 26** — slow, shows a longer picture.
- Fast above slow — uptrend.
- Fast below slow — downtrend.

## Be careful

Moving averages lag: they confirm what has already happened. In a sideways market, when the price swings back and forth, EMAs cross often and give false signals.
