## What a candle is

A Japanese candlestick shows how the price moved over a period of time:

- **Open** — the price at the start of the period.
- **Close** — the price at the end.
- **High** and **Low** — the maximum and minimum within the period (the "wicks").

A green candle closed above its open, the price went up. A red one closed below its open.

## Timeframe

The **timeframe** is how much time one candle covers: 1 minute, 5 minutes, an hour, a day. The game chart uses 5-minute candles — the same ones the AI personas were trained on.

## Noise and signal

The smaller the timeframe, the more random moves. One long red 5m candle says nothing about the daily trend. Before acting, look at what dozens of candles are doing.

## Long wicks

A long upper wick means someone tried to push the price up but sellers brought it back down. A long lower wick is the opposite. It is a hint, not a guarantee.
