## What MACD is

**MACD** is the difference between the fast (12) and slow (26) EMA. If you took the trend lesson, you already know its basis.

- MACD above zero — the fast EMA is above the slow one, momentum is up.
- MACD below zero — momentum is down.

## Signal line

Next to it there is usually a **signal line** — a 9-candle EMA of MACD itself. MACD crossing the signal line upward is taken as a sign that momentum is turning up, crossing downward — down.

## Histogram

Histogram bars are the distance between MACD and the signal line. Growing bars mean momentum is strengthening, shrinking bars mean it is fading — even while the price is still moving the same way.

## Limits

MACD, like the EMA, lags. In a sideways market it gives many false crossings. Use it as confirmation, not as the only reason for a trade.
